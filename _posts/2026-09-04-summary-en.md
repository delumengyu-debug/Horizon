## [OpenAI Unveils GPT-6 Astra With System Card Amid Benchmark Debate](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/) ⭐️ 10.0/10

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra, releasing a system card on its deployment safety hub. The launch is accompanied by community analyses of the model's results on ARC-AGI-3 and the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. This is a major frontier-model release that could reset expectations for AI capabilities and safety transparency. The unusually strong community reaction shows that benchmark methodology and the definition of progress toward AGI are now central topics of debate. The system card is available at deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra. Commenters note the ARC-AGI-3 scorecard lists 7.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol but estimates roughly 30% with the Responses API harness, raising fairness concerns; one chart shows GPT-6 Astra significantly outperforming Fable 5.1.

**Background**: A system card is an OpenAI transparency document describing a deployed model's capabilities, limitations, and safety evaluations. ARC-AGI-3 is an interactive reasoning benchmark that challenges AI agents to explore novel environments and learn continuously, while the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index ranks models on real-world coding tasks. These evaluations are widely used to gauge progress among frontier AI models.
